WHAT YOU GET

Everything you need to practice

  • 1,808 multiple-choice questions across 6 domains and 52 topics
  • 3 levels: Easy, Medium and Hard. Pick topics, level and set size (5, 10 or 20)
  • Full notes: the answer, why each other choice is wrong, the key concept, an IOS example and a tip
  • Missed questions come back until you get them right
  • Unlimited subnetting, generated and checked by the app
  • Your stats: accuracy by domain and level, plus weak topics
  • Levels, daily quests and badges to keep you going
  • English or Korean interface. Questions are in English, like the real exam
HOW IT WORKS

STEP 2

Each answer is checked right away, with notes for every choice.

STEP 3

Review and retry

See your results, then retry the ones you missed. Watch your weak topics shrink.
